# Video-Game-Trends--Data-Science-Project
This was a group project where we analyzed a large dataset of video games to explore what factors contribute to a game becoming a commercial hit. Using Python, Pandas, Seaborn, and Matplotlib, we cleaned the data, handled missing values, and created visualizations—including scatterplots, bar graphs, and correlation analyses to identify relationships between variables. Our findings helped us draw data driven conclusions about trends in the gaming industry and what characteristics are most common in successful works.

My personal contributions include: 

Helping define the research objectives and analytical focus of the project

Creating the scatterplot and bar graph to visualize key relationships in the dataset

Assisting in interpreting the results and forming final conclusions
